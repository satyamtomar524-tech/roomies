RoomMate brings your room map and your shopping notes into the same workspace. Here is what happens, and what you need to check yourself.

01

Start with measurements.

The first room is a labeled 300 × 350 cm sample. Its area is 10.5 m². Enter your own wall dimensions before relying on placements. Measure the full bed frame, desk, and wardrobe, including parts that stick out.

Room settings hold the dimensions and the positions of doors, windows, and fixed obstacles. Confirm the measurements only after checking the actual room; a sample room cannot produce buying alerts. Wall and floor colours give a schematic preview, not a material or lighting simulation.

The plan uses the upper-left corner as its origin: X goes right, Y goes down. A position of X = 20, Y = 100 starts 20 cm from the left wall and 100 cm from the top wall.

Dragging and numeric controls update the same coordinates. Rotation is 0° or 90°. RoomMate saves locally and shows whether the save succeeded.

02

Separate what you own from what comes later.

Owned floor furniture uses a solid outline. A planned item uses a dashed outline and reserves its space. Give it the largest dimensions you are comfortable buying.

A floor item occupies space. A surface item, such as a lamp, belongs to a supporting desk or cabinet. Its position is measured inside that surface. An overlay, such as a rug, can sit beneath furniture. Wall items are marked separately and do not occupy floor area.

Overlap, room-boundary, fixed-obstacle, and inward-door checks flag measured conflicts. Preferred clearance is your setting, not a building standard. Suggested positions use a small search and explain the rule behind the option; they do not prove that a whole room is optimal.

03

Give every find a reserved place.

Add the exact product link, its dimensions, the chosen size or finish, and a delivered-price target. Link it to the planned item it would replace. The fit check compares the product with that reservation, including the allowed 90° orientation.

For automatic price-drop notices, enter the retailer SKU and confirm the saved variant. The fetched SKU, requested link and current product identity must match. A name alone does not confirm the variant.

If the dimensions or variant are not confirmed, the result stays incomplete. A product being smaller than a slot does not prove assembly access, load capacity, comfort, or suitability for its intended use.

04

Keep the evidence with the price.

Record the item price, shipping, stock, exact variant, source link, and time. Enter zero shipping only when it is confirmed free. A blank shipping amount means unknown.

The journal keeps observations instead of overwriting the past. Delivered price is item price plus confirmed shipping. An in-app target alert requires a confirmed room and fit, an available exact variant, a fresh observation, and a delivered price at or below your target. Missing facts stay visible.

A lower item price can produce a separately labeled informational notice. Unknown delivery still prevents a delivered-price recommendation. The first quote and an unchanged price do not create a drop notice.

A live check reads public structured Product and Offer data on supported pages and leaves delivery unknown. It does not sign in, use your shopping account, or bypass blocked pages. Pages with multiple offers, missing data, or unsupported currencies need a manual observation. Scheduled checks run only while the local server is open and the product's monitoring is enabled.

RoomMate helps you decide. You open the retailer link and make the final purchase yourself.

05

Follow one action through the code.

Browser interface→Python API→Fit and price rules→SQLite records

web/app.js handles the visible controls and sends JSON requests. roommate/server.py receives them. geometry.py checks the room and placements. pricing.py checks products and price evidence. storage.py keeps the room, products, observations, and notifications.

When you move a desk, the browser changes its X and Y, the server validates the data, geometry checks the new placement, and SQLite saves it. A later price check uses that same saved reservation.

The repository's docs/PROJECT_GUIDE.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md explain the files and rules in more detail. Tests in tests/ check the important behaviors. The verification document records what was actually run.

Keep your actual room private.

Your runtime database is excluded from Git. Exported backups can contain measurements, product links, and notes. The public repository contains source and sample fixtures; review any backup before sharing it.
