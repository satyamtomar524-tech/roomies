QUICK GUIDE

One place for the flat.

Pick one task. Your room, belongings, kitchen and shared costs have their own simple pages.

Find my things Kitchen checklist Split a bill Plan my room Track an upgrade
01

Know where things are.

In My flat, add a belonging, its owner, room and exact spot. For example: Kettle → Kitchen → Worktop beside the sink. Choose Shared for things everyone uses.

Search by name or location later. Belongings do not need measurements. Add flatmates in flat settings before assigning an owner.

02

Check the kitchen.

Pans and kettles belong in the flat inventory. Food goes in the Fridge, Freezer or Pantry list. Add an amount such as “1 carton” and mark it Stocked, Low or Out.

Low and Out items form a shopping checklist. Optional best-before dates help organize what to check; the app cannot assess food safety from those dates.

03

Split a shared bill.

In Shared costs, enter the title, date and amount. Select who paid and the people sharing it. Someone can pay for others without taking a share.

Positive balances mean money owed to a person; negative balances mean money they owe. Splits keep every cent: €20.00 divided three ways is €6.67, €6.67 and €6.66.

Once you have paid someone back, choose Record repayment. Check the people, amount and date, then confirm the payment has already been made. Your balance updates; spending stays the same. The app does not send money or verify bank transfers.

Open Rent & regular bills to save rent, internet or another monthly bill. After paying it, choose Record paid bill and review the details. Templates never create charges automatically. Each saved bill can be recorded once per calendar month.

04

Arrange the room.

My room begins with a labeled 300 × 350 cm sample. Enter your actual room, door, window and furniture measurements before relying on it. The flat list and room plan remain separate.

Select an item and drag it, edit X/Y or rotate it. Arrow keys move by 5 cm; R rotates. More details contains the less frequent controls. Solid items are owned; dashed items reserve a future place.

A lamp can sit on a supporting desk. A rug can overlap floor furniture. Room checks flag modeled overlaps, boundaries and access zones. This is a rectangular concept plan, not a professional survey.

05

Check an upgrade before buying.

In Upgrades, link an exact product to a reserved room place. Enter its dimensions and a delivered-price target. Record the price, delivery, stock, source, time and variant check.

Blank delivery means unknown. An item-price change is different from a confirmed delivered-price target alert. Automatic checks work only for compatible public pages; unsupported stores can be recorded manually.

A confirmed saved SKU and matching product link are needed for live variant confidence. Checks run while the local server is open. The sample room cannot produce buying alerts.

Your records stay local.

Member names are labels in one local workspace, not accounts or invitations. Current backups include the room, flat, repayments and monthly bills. Old room-only imports leave the flat unchanged; an older flat backup may clear newer lists. Review the import summary and keep actual backups private.

How the project works

The browser sends JSON to roommate/server.py. household.py validates belongings and kitchen records. expenses.py splits exact cents. geometry.py checks the room. pricing.py checks price evidence. storage.py saves everything in SQLite.

Start with docs/PROJECT_GUIDE.md in the repository, then follow one action through the code. The tests and verification record show what was checked.
